🤔 Cloudflare Hai Kya?

Cloudflare duniya ki sab se bari web-infrastructure companies me se ek hai. Asaan lafzon me — ye tumhari website aur visitors ke beech me ek smart layer lagata hai jo:

  • ⚡ Site tez karta hai (CDN — duniya bhar ke servers se content)
  • 🔒 Hackers se bachata hai (DDoS protection)
  • 🔐 Free SSL certificate deta hai (🔒 wala lock!)

🎁 Free Plan Me Kya-Kya Milta Hai?

  • 🌐 Fast DNS — domain jaldi resolve hota hai
  • ⚡ Global CDN — site duniya bhar me tez khulti hai
  • 🛡️ Unmetered DDoS protection — unlimited attack protection!
  • 🔐 Universal SSL — free HTTPS certificate
  • 📊 Basic analytics — visitors ka hisab

Bonus: Cloudflare Pages se static website free host bhi kar sakte ho (custom domain + unlimited bandwidth ke sath)! Aur Cloudflare Registrar se domain at-cost price par (bina extra markup ke)!

📝 Free Plan Kaise Start Karein? (Step by Step)

Step 1: cloudflare.com/plans kholo 👇

⚠️ 2 Zaroori Baatein (Parh Lo!)

1. 🎯 Free Plan Personal/Hobby Sites Ke Liye Hai
Cloudflare khud kehta hai free plan "personal or hobby projects that aren't business-critical" ke liye hai. Bare business ke liye Pro ($20/mo) behtar hai!
2. 🌐 Nameserver Change Me Waqt Lagta Hai
Domain ke nameservers badalne ke baad kuch ghante lag sakte hain propagate hone me. Ghabrana nahi — normal hai! Is doran site kabhi purane to kabhi naye server se khulegi.
