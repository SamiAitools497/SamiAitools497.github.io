🤔 Ye Offer Hai Kya?

October 2026 me reports aayi ke OpenAI kuch Free-plan users ko 1 month ka ChatGPT Plus trial de raha hai. Pakistan me bhi kuch accounts par ye offer verify hui hai. Offer wale accounts par subscription page par Plus ke neeche “Claim free offer” ka button dikhta hai, aur pehle mahine ki price $0 likhi hoti hai.

Ye offer un accounts ko target karti hai jin ne pehle kabhi paid ChatGPT plan nahi liya. Jin accounts ne pehle Go ya Plus use kiya ho, un par ye offer aksar nahi dikhti. Is liye do doston ke accounts par alag result aa sakta hai — aik ko mile, doosre ko na mile — aur dono theek hain.

✅ Tum Eligible Ho Ya Nahi — Kaise Check Karo?

Step 1: Browser me chatgpt.com kholo aur apne account se login karo. App ke andar se nahi, browser se check karna behtar hai.
Step 2: Upgrade plan ya Subscription page par jao.
Step 3: Plus wale card ko dekho. Agar wahan “Claim free offer” likha hai aur pehle mahine ki price $0 hai, to tum eligible ho.
Step 4: Agar wahan sirf $20/month likha hai aur koi free offer nahi hai, to tumhare account par ye offer nahi aayi hai.

Yaad rakho: Offer na dikhna tumhari ghalti nahi hai, aur is ko zabardasti lane ka koi official tareeqa nahi hai. Koi website ya banda agar “sab ko free Plus dilwa doon ga” ka dawa kare, to wo scam ho sakta hai.

💳 Card Kyun Lazmi Hai? Aur Kaunsa Card Chalega?

Free month ke liye bhi valid payment method add karna lazmi hai. Pehle mahine me paise nahi kat-te, lekin card is liye manga jata hai taake free month khatam hone par subscription khud-ba-khud renew ho sake.

  • ATM/debit card chal sakta hai, agar us par international online payment on ho.
  • Pakistani cards par bank ka rate aur tax alag se lag sakta hai — final amount bank par depend karta hai.
  • Card ki poori detail kabhi kisi ko screenshot me mat bhejo, aur kisi anjaan website par card mat daalo — sirf chatgpt.com par.

🔁 Auto-Renew Se Kaise Bachen? (Sab Se Zaroori Hissa)

Ye wohi jagah hai jahan log phans jate hain. Trial le liya, maza aaya, phir bhool gaye — aur agle mahine $20 charge ho gaya.

Step 1: Trial lete hi apni renewal date note kar lo — wo Subscription / Billing page par likhi hoti hai.
Step 2: Agar trial ke baad Plus nahi rakhna, to us date se pehle cancel kar do.
Step 3: Cancel karne ke liye browser use karo. Agar subscription website se li gayi ho to app ke andar se change nahi hota — app yehi kehta hai ke “visit chatgpt.com/#settings/Billing in a browser to modify your subscription.”

Cancel karne ke baad Plus usi period ke end tak chalta rehta hai — exact detail Billing page par likhi hoti hai, wahi final samjho.

❌ Kya Nahi Karna?

  • ❌ Cracked, shared ya “saste me lifetime Plus” wale accounts mat lo — wo band ho sakte hain aur account ka risk hai.
  • ❌ Kisi ko apna ChatGPT password, OTP ya card detail mat do.
  • ❌ Ye mat samjho ke link me apna naam daal kar tumhe commission milega — ChatGPT ka koi public affiliate program nahi hai.
  • ❌ Offer ka screenshot dekh kar ye mat samjho ke tumhe bhi zaroor milegi — ye account par depend karta hai.
