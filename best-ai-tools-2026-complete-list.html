🎨 Image Generation Tools (Tasveer Banane Wale)

1. Adobe Firefly 3

Adobe ka apna AI image tool — Photoshop ke sath best kaam karta hai. Designers ke liye banaya gaya hai.

🔗 firefly.adobe.com
2. MidJourney V7

Duniya ka sab se mashhoor AI image generator! Text likho, kamal ki artistic tasveer pao. Discord se chalta hai.

🔗 midjourney.com
3. Stable Diffusion 3.5

Free aur open-source! Apne computer par chalao — koi limit nahi, poora control tumhare hath mein.

🔗 stablediffusionweb.com
4. Leonardo AI

Game art aur character design ke liye best. Artists aur game developers ki favorite choice.

🔗 leonardo.ai
5. Ideogram 3.0

Jis tasveer mein text likhna ho (poster, logo) — is mein sab se sahi spelling aati hai!

🔗 ideogram.ai
6. FLUX.1

Bohat realistic photos banata hai — detail itni ke asli lagti hai. Quality mein top class.

🔗 fluxai.com
7. Reve Image

Naya lekin tezi se mashhoor hota tool — aesthetic aur artistic images ke liye.

🔗 reveai.com
8. Recraft V3

Vector art, logos aur illustrations ke liye perfect — designers ke liye khaas.

🔗 recraft.ai
9. Freepik AI

Stock photos wali mashhoor site ka AI — design assets ke sath image generation.

🔗 freepik.com/ai
10. Imagen 4 (Google)

Google ka latest image model — photorealistic results, seedhi Google quality!

🔗 imagen.research.google
