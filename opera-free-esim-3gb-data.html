📱 Opera Ka FREE eSIM: 3GB Data Kaise Claim Karein? (2026 Guide)

📅 October 2026 · Sami AI Tools Blog

Bahir mulk safar par jana hai aur roaming ke bhaari bills se dar lagta hai? Opera ne iska hal nikaal diya hai! 🎉 Opera for Android browser mein ab built-in travel eSIM aa gaya hai — aur naye users ko 3GB data bilkul FREE (3 din ke liye) mil raha hai!

Is guide mein janiye ke ye offer kya hai, claim kaise karein, aur kin cheezon ka khayal rakhein. Chaliye shuru karte hain! 🚀

✨ Ye Offer Hai Kya?

Opera pehla browser hai jis mein eSIM directly built-in hai. Iska matlab:

  • 📶 3GB FREE data — 3 din ke liye, bilkul muft!
  • 🌍 47 destinations mein chalta hai
  • 📱 Poore phone ko internet milta hai — sirf browser ko nahi!
  • 💳 No payment, no email — claim karne ke liye kuch nahi chahiye!
  • 🔄 Ek hi eSIM multiple countries mein kaam karta hai

📝 Step-by-Step: FREE Data Claim Karein

Step 1: Opera for Android Download/Update Karein
Play Store se Opera for Android download karein ya purana version update karein. ⚠️ Yaad rakhein: ye feature sirf Opera for Android mein hai — Opera Mini ya Opera GX mein nahi!
Step 2: Profile Icon Dabao
Browser kholein aur neeche right corner mein profile icon par tap karein, phir neeche scroll karein.
Step 3: eSIM Option Chunein
Menu mein "eSIM" ka option milega — us par tap karein.
Step 4: "Claim Now" Dabao
"Claim now" ka button dabayein — bas!
Step 5: Country Chunein Aur Confirm Karein
Apni destination country chunein aur confirm karein. Phir phone par eSIM install karne ka prompt aayega — use follow karein! ✅
💡 Pro Tip: eSIM ghar par hi set up kar lein (stable WiFi par)! Data ki counting tabhi shuru hogi jab aap destination country mein network se connect honge. Ek saal tak use na karein to expire ho jata hai!

📋 Kya Chahiye Hoga? (Requirements)

  • 📱 Android 10 ya naya version
  • 🔌 Phone mein eSIM support ho (zyada tar naye phones mein hota hai)
  • 🔓 Phone carrier unlocked ho
  • 🌐 Setup ke liye stable internet (ghar ka WiFi behtar hai)
⚠️ Imaandaar Note: FREE 3GB offer 11 mulkon ke users ke liye hai (US, UK, Germany, France, Canada, UAE, Brazil, Netherlands, Norway, Sweden, Poland). Pakistan is list mein nahi hai — lekin eSIM khareedna sab ke liye available hai, aur 47 destinations mein chalta hai!

💰 FREE Ke Baad Kya? (Paid Plans)

Agar 3GB khatam ho jaye ya zyada data chahiye to paid top-up plans bhi hain:

  • 📦 3GB / 5GB / 10GB — 30 din ke liye valid
  • 💵 Qeemat destination ke hisaab se mukhtalif hoti hai
  • 🔁 Wahi eSIM top-up ho jata hai — naya install nahi karna parta!

❓ Aam Sawalat

Kya mera phone number change hoga?
Nahi! Ye eSIM sirf data ke liye hai — aapka number wahi rahega! 📞

Kya ye sirf browser mein chalega?
Nahi! Poore phone ko internet milta hai — Maps, WhatsApp, sab chalega! 🗺️

Kitne countries mein kaam karta hai?
47 destinations — Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore waghera sab included!

iPhone par chalega?
Abhi ye feature sirf Android par hai — iOS ke bare mein Opera ne kuch nahi kaha!

Opera ka eSIM travelers ke liye game-changer hai — airport par SIM ki line bhool jayein! ✈️ Agar aap eligible country mein hain to FREE 3GB zaroor claim karein. Aur Pakistan se hain to bhi paid plans safar ko aasaan bana sakte hain!
