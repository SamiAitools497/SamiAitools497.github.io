🎥 CapCut Hai Kya?

CapCut ByteDance (TikTok wali company) ki free video editing app hai — phone aur computer dono par chalti hai. Auto-captions, trendy effects, transitions aur templates ki wajah se ye Pakistan ke content creators ka pehla choice ban chuki hai. 🇵🇰

🆓 Free Version Mein Kya Milta Hai?

  • ✂️ Basic editing — trim, cut, merge, speed control
  • 🎨 Bohat se free effects aur transitions
  • 📝 Auto-captions (limited languages/minutes)
  • 🎵 Free music library
  • 📤 HD export (kuch limits ke saath)

Seedhi baat: shuruaat ke liye free version kaafi hai — casual Reels aur TikToks ke liye koi pareshani nahi. 👍

👑 CapCut Pro Mein Kya Extra Milta Hai?

  • ☁️ 100GB cloud storage — projects kahin se bhi kholo
  • ✨ Premium effects, transitions aur filters — jo free mein locked hain
  • 🤖 AI features — advanced auto-captions, background remove, upscale
  • 🚫 No watermark — kuch premium templates par watermark khatam
  • 📦 Commercial use rights — client work ke liye safe
  • 🎞️ 4K export aur zyada export options
💡 Tip: Agar tum clients ke liye videos banate ho ya roz content upload karte ho, to Pro tumhara waqt bachata hai — premium templates se editing aadhi ho jati hai! ⚡
